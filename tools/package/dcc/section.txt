DORITOS CRASH COURSE
==========================================================
Your copy: the Xbox Live Arcade package (in an archive or a folder). The title update is left out.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\DoritosCrashCourse

AVATARS
Everyone in this game is an Xbox Avatar: you, the other runners and the crowd. On the console
their clothes, hair and faces come from the console itself, not from the game. Here they come
from the console's avatar data that the Kinect Sports disc carries: add Kinect Sports to the
launcher too (you don't need to play it) and Doritos Crash Course has its avatars. Without it the
game still plays, but your runner is invisible.

CONTROLS
An Xbox controller, or the keyboard standing in for one:
  Run (left stick)                      A / D  (W / S)
  Jump (A)                              Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Skip ahead when stuck (RB)            R
  Pause (Start)                         Esc

In menus: Space or Enter accepts (A), V or Backspace goes back (B). Starting a level asks to sign
in to Xbox LIVE: press B to continue without it. To play with a controller, open F2 > Players and
set Player 1 to "Controller".
