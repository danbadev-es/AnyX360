KINECT ADVENTURES
==========================================================
Played with a webcam in place of the Kinect.
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files. The "Exclusive Pre-order Pack" downloadable content is installed if it is next to it.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\KinectAdventures

THE WEBCAM
Set it up as for Dance Central 3 (see its section): Windows must let desktop programs use the
camera, the webcam goes under or over your screen pointing at where you play, and you stand
2-3 m away with all of you in view. Kinect Adventures needs more room than dancing: you jump,
duck, lean and step to the sides, so leave space around you.

If you jump and the game doesn't react, press F2 > Kinect: the picture shows your skeleton over
what the camera sees. Check that all of you, feet included, is in it.

PLAYING
- Menus: raise a hand and the cursor follows it; hold it over an item until the circle fills.
- Rallyball: hit the balls with your hands, feet, head or body.
- River Rush: jump to start and to jump, lean or step to the sides to steer the raft.
- Reflex Ridge: jump, duck and step to dodge the obstacles.
- Space Pop: flap your arms to fly up, move around the room to pop the bubbles.
- 20,000 Leaks: plug the leaks with your hands, feet and head.
- Pause: hold your left arm out and down at 45 degrees, away from your body, with the right arm
  relaxed, for a moment (the Kinect Guide gesture). The pause menu has "Leave activity".

KNOWN ISSUES
- There are no avatars: the game shows its stand-ins instead of your Xbox avatar.
- Reflex Ridge and Space Pop haven't been tried by a person yet: if they don't start ("grab
  bars", "flap your arms to start"), tell us with the logs.
- No voice, no online play, no uploading photos.
- The webcam limits are the same as in Dance Central 3: no depth camera, a little more delay
  than a Kinect.
