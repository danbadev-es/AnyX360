DANCE CENTRAL 2
==========================================================
Played with a webcam in place of the Kinect, like Dance Central 3 (see its section for setting up
the webcam, the F2 > Kinect settings and the menu gestures).
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder), and its title update (the TU_
file next to it, or added alone afterwards): the game needs it (version 0.0.1.5). Its downloadable
content is installed too, but those songs aren't listed yet. The songs need keys\title_keys.txt,
which comes with this release.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\DanceCentral2

MENUS
- Raise your right arm out to the side: the cursor follows your hand. Swipe your arm across your
  body to select; "left hand" items (back) with the left arm.
- An Xbox controller works in the menus too: A selects, B goes back (the game switches to
  controller mode; Back leaves it).
- Pause during a song: a controller's Start, or the Kinect Guide gesture (left arm out and down at
  45 degrees for a moment).

KNOWN ISSUES
- The downloadable songs don't appear in the song list yet.
- Break It Down, Fitness, Dance Battle and two players haven't been tried yet.
- Voice commands don't work: there is no microphone support.
- A webcam is not a Kinect: it has no depth camera, so the depth is estimated.
