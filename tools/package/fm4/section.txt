FORZA MOTORSPORT 4 (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the first
disc's files. The second disc's content (the car packs and Autovista, as content packages) and
the downloadable content next to it are installed too. One player; Kinect and split screen
aren't available yet.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\ForzaMotorsport4

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse, standing in for a controller. A controller is recommended: open
F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

KNOWN ISSUES
- In testing: the start, the introduction, the first race, the first car, the career menus and
  World Tour races work, and the game saves. Free Play, the garage, upgrades, tuning, the paint
  editor, replays and the second disc's cars haven't been checked yet. If something goes wrong,
  tell us with the logs.
- Xbox LIVE (Rivals online, the storefront, the auction house) is gone.
