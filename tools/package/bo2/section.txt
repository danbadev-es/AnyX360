CALL OF DUTY: BLACK OPS II (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files, and its title update (the 000B0000 package in the same archive, or added alone afterwards):
the game needs it (version 0.0.18.14). Its map packs are installed too. The campaign, multiplayer and Zombies on one machine (split screen); Xbox LIVE is gone,
so online play isn't available.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\BlackOps2

MULTIPLAYER AND ZOMBIES
Multiplayer and Zombies are a separate program (BlackOps2MP.exe, next to the campaign's):
choosing Multiplayer or Zombies in the main menu closes the game and opens it again in that
mode. In their menus, B (Main menu) offers the campaign and the other mode. The launcher closes
when the game switches; the log keeps going to logs\bo2\last_run.log.
- Local: split screen for up to four players. Players 2-4 use controllers: each presses A in
  the lobby to join (the game asks them to sign in: they choose or create their profile), then
  Start match.
- Bots: Local > Adjust bots adds allied and enemy bots, the local version of Combat Training.
- Zombies > Local: TranZit (Green Run, Bus Depot) alone or with friends; in Town, Grief (two
  players or more, team against team).
- The downloadable maps: Local > Game setup > Change map > DLC (Nuketown 2025, Revolution's,
  Uprising's, Vengeance's and Apocalypse's maps); in Zombies, the globe (RIGHT turns it) offers
  Die Rise, Mob of the Dead, Buried, Origins and Nuketown besides Green Run.
- Xbox LIVE needs Xbox LIVE; System Link asks for a network connection.

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse, standing in for a controller. To play with a controller, open
F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller". Players 2-4: controllers.

  Move / Look                           W A S D / Mouse
  Aim / Fire (LT / RT)                  Right click / Left click
  Jump (A)                              Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Crouch, prone (B)                     V  (also Backspace)
  Reload, use, buy (X)                  E
  Switch weapon (Y)                     F
  Tactical grenade (LB)                 Q
  Lethal grenade (RB)                   R
  Sprint (L3)                           C
  Melee (R3)                            Middle click
  Scorestreaks (D-pad)                  Arrows, 1-4
  Pause (Start)                         Esc
  Scores (Back)                         Tab

In menus: Space or Enter accepts, V or Backspace goes back. The game's text is in the languages
your copy carries (the launcher's Players and language chooses among them).

KNOWN ISSUES
- Four-player split screen runs at about 40-50 FPS on the test machine.
- In testing: the campaign's first mission, split screen matches (two to four players, with
  bots), Magma (Uprising) with two players, TranZit, Grief and Die Rise with two players have
  been played; the rest hasn't been tried yet.
