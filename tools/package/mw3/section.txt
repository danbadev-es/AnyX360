CALL OF DUTY: MODERN WARFARE 3 (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files, and its title update (the 000B0000 package in the same archive, or added alone afterwards):
the game needs it (version 0.0.23.8). Its map packs are installed too. The campaign, Special Ops and multiplayer on one machine (split screen); Xbox LIVE is
gone, so online play isn't available.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\ModernWarfare3

MULTIPLAYER
Multiplayer is a separate program (ModernWarfare3MP.exe, next to the campaign's): choosing
Multiplayer in the main menu closes the game and opens it again in multiplayer, and its Main
Menu entry goes back to the campaign. The launcher closes when the game switches; the log
keeps going to logs\mw3\last_run.log.
- Split Screen: up to four players on one screen. Players 2-4 use controllers: each presses A
  in their corner to join (as a guest; X then signs them in: they choose or create their
  profile), then everyone presses A to continue, and Start Match.
- Special Ops: Split Screen in its menu plays Survival with a second player the same way (the
  missions haven't been tried yet).
- The downloadable maps (Content Collections 1-4, Terminal, Overwatch...) are in Game Setup >
  Map > Bonus maps.
- Xbox LIVE says to sign in to Xbox LIVE; System Link finds no games.

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse, standing in for a controller. To play with a controller, open
F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller". Players 2-4: controllers.

  Move / Look                           W A S D / Mouse
  Aim / Fire (LT / RT)                  Right click / Left click
  Jump (A)                              Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Crouch, prone (B)                     V  (also Backspace)
  Reload, use (X)                       E
  Switch weapon (Y)                     F
  Tactical grenade (LB)                 Q
  Frag grenade (RB)                     R
  Sprint (L3)                           C
  Melee (R3)                            Middle click
  Pause (Start)                         Esc
  Objectives (Back)                     Tab

In menus: Space or Enter accepts, V or Backspace goes back. The game's text is in the languages
your copy carries (the launcher's Players and language chooses among them).

KNOWN ISSUES
- About one start in fifteen stays on a black screen after the first frame, more while the computer
  is busy with other programs: close the game and start it again.
- Split screen runs below 60 FPS in matches on slower machines.
- In testing: the menus, the campaign's opening, split screen matches on Seatown (two and
  three players), a two-player match on Piazza (a downloadable map) and Survival on Resistance
  have been played; four players haven't been tried yet.
- In the intro level the snowy trees may look flat white.
