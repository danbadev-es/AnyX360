AnyX360 - Gears of War 3 · version {VERSION}
==========================================================
Developed by danbadev.                         (Español: LEEME.txt)

Xbox 360's Gears of War 3, recompiled to run natively on Windows.
This folder does NOT include the game: you need your own legal copy of Gears of War 3
(the .7z archive with the disc).

By using this software you accept the terms of use in TERMS.txt, which the launcher shows
you the first time you open it. The changes in each version are listed in CHANGELOG.md.


REQUIREMENTS
- 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
- A DirectX 12 graphics card with up-to-date drivers.
- About 8 GB free on the drive where this folder is (the game is extracted into it).
- The .7z archive of your copy of the game ("Gears Of War 3.7z").


FIRST TIME
1. Extract this .zip into a regular folder, for example Documents or D:\Games
   (not inside "Program Files").
2. Open "AnyX360 Gears of War 3.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway" (the program is not digitally signed).
   The first time you will see the terms of use: read and accept them to continue.
   The launcher is in English and Spanish: switch the language in the window's footer.
3. Click "Choose .7z archive…" and select the game's .7z. It is extracted into the "game"
   folder (a few minutes). You can delete the .7z afterwards if you want.
4. Click "Play".

The first game may stutter a little while the graphics are prepared; then it runs smoothly.


CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse. Player 2: Xbox controller.
To play alone with a controller, open F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

  Move                                  W A S D
  Look                                  Mouse
  Aim / Fire                            Right click / Left click
  Cover, roadie run, evade (A)          Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Melee, chainsaw (B)                   V  (also Backspace)
  Reload (RB)                           R
  Use, pick up, revive (X)              E
  Point of interest (Y)                 F
  Tac-Com (LB)                          Q
  Switch weapon (D-pad)                 Arrows, 1 2 3 4 or mouse wheel
  Scope zoom (R3)                       Middle click
  L3                                    C
  Pause (Start)                         Esc
  Scoreboard (Back)                     Tab

In menus: Space or Enter accepts, V or Backspace goes back.


PROGRAM KEYS
  F1                  Statistics (FPS, memory)
  F2                  Settings: resolution, fullscreen, upscaling, 30/60 FPS, VSync,
                      mouse sensitivity and each player's device
  F11 or Alt+Enter    Fullscreen
  Alt+Tab             Releases the mouse


SPLIT SCREEN (local co-op)
With a controller connected, player 2 presses Start in the main menu to join the party.
Then go into Campaign as usual: the screen splits in two.


SAVED GAMES AND SETTINGS
They are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\GearsOfWar3 (paste it into File Explorer's address
bar). They are kept when you move to a new version.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- If the game closes with an error, the launcher tells you. Click "Logs" and send the
  last_run.log file (and previous_run.log if there is one), with the version ({VERSION})
  and what you were doing.
- If it freezes, close it and send last_run.log anyway.


KNOWN ISSUES
- Cinematics and videos run at 30 FPS: that is how they are in the original game.
- In Horde, the counter before the first wave may stay at 0:30.
- The game may occasionally close while loading Horde maps.
- There is no online play (the Xbox 360's Xbox Live no longer exists). Campaign, private
  Horde and split screen work.
