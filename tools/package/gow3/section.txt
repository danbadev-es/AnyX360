GEARS OF WAR 3
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package with its title update (0.0.6.7), for example
"GOW3-DLC-TU-GOD.rar". Its downloadable content is installed with it.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\GearsOfWar3

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse. Player 2: Xbox controller.
To play alone with a controller, open F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

  Move                                  W A S D
  Look                                  Mouse
  Aim / Fire                            Right click / Left click
  Cover, roadie run, evade (A)          Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Melee, chainsaw (B)                   V  (also Backspace)
  Reload (RB)                           R
  Use, pick up, revive (X)              E
  Point of interest (Y)                 F
  Tac-Com (LB)                          Q
  Switch weapon (D-pad)                 Arrows, 1 2 3 4 or mouse wheel
  Scope zoom (R3)                       Middle click
  L3                                    C
  Pause (Start)                         Esc
  Scoreboard (Back)                     Tab

In menus: Space or Enter accepts, V or Backspace goes back.

SPLIT SCREEN (local co-op)
With a controller connected, player 2 presses Start in the main menu to join the party.
Then go into Campaign as usual: the screen splits in two.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Cinematics and videos run at 30 FPS: that is how they are in the original game.
- In Horde, the counter before the first wave stays at 0:30 until a Command Post is built:
  hold Y (F) at a green ghost tower.
- The game may occasionally close while loading Horde maps.
- Online play doesn't work; campaign, private Horde and split screen do.
