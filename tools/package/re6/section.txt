RESIDENT EVIL 6 (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files, and its title update (the TU_ file next to it, or added alone afterwards): the game needs
it (version 0.0.6.3). Its downloadable content is installed too (the voice packs, the
Mercenaries stages, Siege Game Mode and the 3-Mode Pack). One player: split screen and online
play aren't available.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\ResidentEvil6

The first time, the game starts Leon and Helena's prologue straight from the title screen; the
main menu (the campaigns, Mercenaries) opens once the prologue is finished.

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse, standing in for a controller. To play with a controller, open
F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

  Move / Camera                         W A S D / Mouse
  Ready weapon / Fire (LT / RT)         Right click / Left click (without the weapon ready: melee)
  Run, action, doors, vault (A)         Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Partner action (B)                    V  (also Backspace)
  Pick up, reload (X)                   E
  Item menu, herbs (Y)                  F
  Show the way (LB)                     Q
  Health tablet (RB)                    R
  L3 / R3                               C / Middle click
  Weapons and items (D-pad)             Arrows, 1-4, mouse wheel
  Pause / Options (Start / Back)        Esc / Tab

Timed prompts (a needle turning round a button) want the button while the needle is inside the
coloured arc; the stick prompt wants the movement keys pressed in turn (W, D, S, A).

KNOWN ISSUES
- In testing: the start, the saves and the prologue up to the street after the alley have been
  played with a test player; the end of the prologue and what comes after it haven't been seen
  yet. If something goes wrong, tell us with the logs.
- It runs at 30 FPS, as on the console, with short stutters the first time an area appears.
