MINECRAFT: XBOX 360 EDITION
==========================================================
Your copy: the Xbox Live Arcade package (in an archive or a folder) and its title update (the
584111F7\000B0000 package next to it, or added alone afterwards): the game needs it. The
downloadable content packs next to them (skins, texture packs, mash-ups) are installed too.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\Minecraft

CONTROLS
An Xbox controller, or the keyboard and mouse standing in for one:
  Move (left stick)                     W A S D
  Look (right stick)                    Mouse
  Jump (A)                              Space
  Mine / attack (RT)                    Left mouse button
  Place / use (LT)                      Right mouse button
  Change item (LB / RB)                 Q / R
  Inventory (Y), crafting (X)           F, E
  Pause (Start)                         Esc

"Play Game" asks about Xbox LIVE: choose "Continue offline" (the second button).

SPLIT SCREEN (UP TO FOUR PLAYERS)
Each player needs a controller. In a game, the new player presses START: the game says "Press START
to join". Press START again and the screen splits. Players 3 and 4 join the same way. To play
player 1 with a controller too, open F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

KNOWN ISSUES
- The pictures of the worlds in the Load list are black.
- Split screen is heavier: on a slower PC the frame rate drops with three or four players.
- Downloadable content added with an earlier AnyX360 launcher: add it again (the game needs each
  pack's name, which the earlier launcher didn't keep).
- Online games, joining other people's games, the store and leaderboards need Xbox LIVE, which is
  gone.
- Only some of the downloadable content packs have been tried: if something is wrong, tell us
  with the logs.
