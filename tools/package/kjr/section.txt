KINECT JOY RIDE (in testing)
==========================================================
Played with a webcam in place of the Kinect.
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files, and its Title Update #3 (the TU_ file next to it, or added alone afterwards): the game
needs it. The "Kinect Joy Ride Chevy Volt" and "Chevrolet Car Pack" downloadable content is
installed if it is next to it. Adding the game also installs the console's avatar data its disc
carries.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\KinectJoyRide

THE WEBCAM
Set it up as for Dance Central 3 (see its section): Windows must let desktop programs use the
camera, the webcam goes under or over your screen pointing at where you play, and you stand
2-3 m away with all of you in view. Two players stand side by side, at least a metre apart.

PLAYING
- Menus: raise a hand and the cursor follows it; hold it over an item until the circle fills.
- Steer: hold both hands in front of your chest as if on a steering wheel, and turn it. Grabbing
  the wheel starts the race.
- Turbo: pull your hands back to your chest, then push them forward (at the start, and when the
  turbo bar is full).
- Drift: lean into a turn. Tricks: lean in any direction while the car is in the air.
- A second player joins by jumping in the lobby ("Salta para unirte").
- Pause: hold your left arm out and down at 45 degrees, away from your body, for a moment (the
  Kinect Guide gesture).

KNOWN ISSUES
- In testing: menus, races (Pro Race), Battle Race and two-player races have been tried with a
  test player, not yet by a person with a webcam. If something is wrong, tell us with the logs.
- It runs at 30 FPS, as on the console (the 60 FPS setting doesn't apply to it).
- You drive as the default avatar (there is no Xbox profile with yours).
- Online play and avatar awards need Xbox Live, which is gone.
- Same webcam limits as Dance Central 3.
