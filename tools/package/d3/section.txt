DIABLO III: REAPER OF SOULS - ULTIMATE EVIL EDITION (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder), and its title update (the
394F07D4\000B0000 package next to it, or added alone afterwards): the game needs it (version
0.0.2.14). Its downloadable content is installed too.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\Diablo3

CONTROLS
Played with a controller, as on the console. One to four players on the same screen: give each
player a controller in F2 > Players. A new player presses a button on their controller, then
chooses or creates their hero.
