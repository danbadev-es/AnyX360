FRUIT NINJA KINECT
==========================================================
Played with a webcam in place of the Kinect.
Your copy: the Xbox Live Arcade package (in an archive or a folder). The downloadable content next
to it (blades, backgrounds, shadows) is installed too; the title update is left out.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\FruitNinjaKinect

THE WEBCAM
Set it up as for Dance Central 3 (see its section): Windows must let desktop programs use the
camera, the webcam goes under or over your screen pointing at where you play, and you stand
2-3 m away. Your head and both hands must stay in view as you swing your arms.

The title screen says "CALIBRATING" for a moment: on the console it would aim the Kinect. Here
nothing moves; frame yourself with the webcam instead (F2 > Kinect shows what it sees).

PLAYING
- Your hands are blades: swipe through a fruit to slice it. Your shadow is behind the fruit.
- Menus: slice an item's fruit to choose it (the watermelon on the title screen to begin).
- Arcade: 60 seconds; slice the special bananas for Freeze, Frenzy and Double Score.
- Classic: three missed fruit and the round ends; don't touch the bombs.
- Zen: 90 seconds without bombs.
- Party: two players at once.
- Kinect Tuner: Y on a controller.
- Pause: hold your left arm out and down at 45 degrees, away from your body, for a moment (the
  Kinect Guide gesture).

KNOWN ISSUES
- Leaderboards ("Connect to Xbox Live") and avatar awards need Xbox Live, which is gone.
- Zen and Party modes haven't been tried by a person yet: if something is wrong, tell us with
  the logs.
- The webcam limits are the same as in Dance Central 3: no depth camera, a little more delay
  than a Kinect.
