CALL OF DUTY: BLACK OPS III (in testing)
==========================================================
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files, its title update (the 000B0000 package in the same archive, or added alone afterwards: the
game needs it, version 0.0.8.5) and its downloadable content. The Xbox 360 version has no campaign
and no split screen: multiplayer and Zombies for one player; Xbox LIVE is gone, so online play
isn't available.
Multiplayer needs the Compatibility Packs (downloadable content, free on the console): without
them the game asks to download them from Xbox LIVE and can't start multiplayer. Add the copy
with them.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\BlackOps3

MULTIPLAYER AND ZOMBIES
Zombies is a separate program (BlackOps3ZM.exe, next to the multiplayer one): choosing Zombies
in the LOCAL menu closes the game and opens it again in Zombies (about a minute). The launcher
closes when the game switches; the log keeps going to logs\bo3\last_run.log.
- Multiplayer > Start match: a local match (Team Deathmatch on Combine by default). Game setup
  changes the map, the mode and the rules; Adjust bots sets each team's size (bots fill it) and
  their difficulty.
- Zombies > Play > Start match: Shadows of Evil.

CONTROLS
Player 1: keyboard and mouse, standing in for a controller. To play with a controller, open
F2 > Players and set Player 1 to "Controller".

  Move / Look                           W A S D / Mouse
  Aim / Fire (LT / RT)                  Right click / Left click
  Jump (A)                              Space  (also Left Shift or Enter)
  Crouch, slide (B)                     V  (also Backspace)
  Reload, use, buy (X)                  E
  Switch weapon (Y)                     F
  Tactical / lethal (LB / RB)           Q / R
  Sprint (L3)                           C
  Melee (R3)                            Middle click
  Scorestreaks (D-pad)                  Arrows, 1-4
  Pause (Start)                         Esc
  Scores (Back)                         Tab

In menus: Space or Enter accepts, V or Backspace goes back. The game's text is in the languages
your copy carries (the launcher's Players and language chooses among them).

KNOWN ISSUES
- In testing: local multiplayer matches on Combine (with bots) and Shadows of Evil have been
  played; the downloadable maps (The Giant, Der Eisendrache, Awakening's), Free Run and the other
  modes haven't been tried yet. If something goes wrong, tell us with the logs.
- Split screen isn't available: this version of the game has one local player.
