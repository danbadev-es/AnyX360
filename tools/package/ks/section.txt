KINECT SPORTS (in testing)
==========================================================
Played with a webcam in place of the Kinect.
Your copy: the Games on Demand package (in an archive or a folder) or a folder with the disc's
files. Adding it also installs the console's avatar data the disc carries, which Doritos Crash
Course uses too.
Saved games and settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnyX360\KinectSports

THE WEBCAM
Set it up as for Dance Central 3 (see its section): Windows must let desktop programs use the
camera, the webcam goes under or over your screen pointing at where you play, and you stand
2-3 m away with all of you in view. Leave room around you: you run, jump, kick and swing.

PLAYING
- Title screen: raise a hand above your head. Then stand in the purple area.
- Menus: raise a hand and the cursor follows it; hold it over an item until the circle fills.
- Pick the main player (yourself, or one of the game's guests), then "Play" in the stadium and a
  sport: Soccer, Bowling, Track & Field, Boxing, Beach Volleyball or Table Tennis. Each sport
  shows how to play it before it starts.
- Kinect Tuner: Y on a controller.
- Pause: hold your left arm out and down at 45 degrees, away from your body, for a moment (the
  Kinect Guide gesture).

KNOWN ISSUES
- In testing: the menus and the start of Bowling have been tried with a test player; the sports
  haven't been played through by a person yet. If something is wrong, tell us with the logs.
- Avatars: you play as the default avatar (there is no Xbox profile with yours). The avatars'
  face shapes (chin, nose, ears) aren't applied, and a tight piece of clothing can show the body
  through it.
- Online play and avatar awards need Xbox Live, which is gone.
- Same webcam limits as Dance Central 3.
